Sinking Tiles

Every tile you touch sinks. Keep moving.

Layers of tiles float above each other. Every tile you step on turns grey and drops away, even if you stand still. Fall through a hole and you land on the layer below. Fall through the bottom layer and you are out.

Play it

Sinking Tiles is a whole room, not a game you add to your own world. Open it as a space in VR or on desktop:

  • Altspace: type sink.firer.at into the search bar.
  • Banter: open https://sink.firer.at as a space.

The game runs entirely in the space, so there is no server and nothing to install.

How to play

  1. Press JOIN GAME. The game starts 10 seconds after the first player joins.
  2. Every tile you stand on turns grey and drops away, even if you stand still.
  3. Keep moving! Fall through a hole and you land on the layer below.
  4. Fall through the bottom layer and you are out.
  5. The last player standing wins. Playing alone? Survive as long as you can.

Controls

The control panels are in the lobby. Host controls only show for the host, and anyone can claim host.

  • Layers: 1, 3 or 5 layers of tiles (lobby only).
  • Tiles: big 8x8 tiles or small 12x12 tiles (lobby only).
  • Sink: how fast a tile drops after you step on it.
  • Start now: skip the countdown. Reset: end the game.
  • Claim host: take over if the host has left.
  • Sound: turn the game sounds on or off, just for you.

Scoreboard

The scoreboard in the lobby keeps most wins, best survival time and most falls. Scores stay with the room.
